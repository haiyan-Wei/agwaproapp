What is AGWA?

AGWA uses widely available GIS data layers to set up, run, and map KINEROS2 watershed simulations. It helps researchers, land managers, and planners understand how land cover change, wildfire, and management decisions affect runoff and erosion. The latest version is built in Python/ArcPy for ArcGIS Pro.

It is developed by the USDA-ARS Southwest Watershed Research Center and the University of Arizona. Earlier versions were also developed with the U.S. EPA Office of Research and Development and the University of Wyoming.

Key features

Watershed setup

Delineate, discretize, and parameterize watersheds using public or your own data.

KINEROS2 simulation

Run event-based hydrologic and erosion models. RHEM is included for hillslope-scale rangeland simulations.

Precipitation inputs

Build rainfall files from your own inputs or from the NOAA Precipitation Frequency Database.

Pre- and post-fire modeling

Compare watershed response before and after wildfire to support burned-area assessment.

Management scenarios

Test land cover modifications and stock pond management to see how they change outcomes.

Visualization

Map results and compare scenarios side by side directly in ArcGIS Pro.
